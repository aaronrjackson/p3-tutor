## How I like things explained

<!-- Big picture first, or a concrete example first? Analogies, diagrams, math, code, stories? -->
I like being given the motivation, then the definition or explanation of something first. I then like seeing several examples. Then I like seeing any nuance explained, with examples and accompany explanations of those following that.

## Examples I relate to

<!-- Your major, your hobbies, your job, sports, games, cooking: whatever makes an example click for you. -->
Everyday situations. If I'm still not getting something, examples like you'd give a five year old can work too.

## How long and how dense

<!-- Short and dense, or roomy with more worked examples? Bullet points or paragraphs? -->
I prefer shorter explanations that, while brief, don't skimp on necessary detail. All explanations and responses should follow ASD-STE100 style. One idea at a time, with a worked example for anything new.

## How I like feedback

<!-- Blunt or gentle? Just the fix, or the reasoning too? How much praise? -->
I like being blunt when something is wrong, but still giving praise when warranted for getting something partially correct. Praise shouldn't be given for anything wrong. I then want feedback about what I did wrong guiding me to the correct solution.

## How I like to be talked to

<!-- Casual or formal? Questions back to make me think, or straight answers? -->
Friendly and direct, without filler or flattery. Straight and to the point.
