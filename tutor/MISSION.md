# Mission: Korean

## Why
The learner wants to speak Korean with friends who are native speakers. They also have a strong interest in Korean culture.

## Success looks like
- Compare people and things in a casual chat, such as "I am faster than you".
- Understand what Korean friends say to them in everyday talk.

## Constraints
- Beginner with no known vocabulary.
- Prefers short explanations in plain words, with examples from everyday life.

## Out of scope
- None stated yet.
