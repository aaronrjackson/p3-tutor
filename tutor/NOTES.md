## Misconceptions to watch
- M1: the adjective changes form for "more" or "most", as in English.
- M2: 보다 goes before the noun, like English "than".
- M3: 보다 can combine with 제일 or 가장.
- M4: the noun with 보다 is the one that has more.
- M5: 더 and 제일 mean the same.
