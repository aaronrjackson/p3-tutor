# Korean Resources

## Knowledge

- [How to Study Korean, Unit 1, Lesson 19](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit1/unit-1-lessons-17-25-2/lesson-19/)
  Free beginner course page on 더, 덜 and 보다 with many examples. Use for: comparatives with 더, 덜 and 보다.
- [Migaku: Korean Comparatives](https://migaku.com/blog/korean/korean-comparatives-how-to-make-comparisons)
  One-page guide to comparatives and superlatives. Use for: the 가장 and 제일 pattern.
- [Sublearn: Making Comparisons](https://sublearn.com/learn/ko/grammar/chapter/making-comparisons)
  Short chapter with practice sentences. Use for: extra practice and the rule against mixing 가장 with 보다.
- [Learn-Korean.net: Comparatives 더 and 가장](https://www.learn-korean.net/lessons/intermediate/comparatives)
  A second view of 더 and 가장. Use for: cross-checking a rule.
